# Blackthorn 404 — Phase 7 Report

**Release:** v0.7.0  
**Phase:** Historical Chapters II–IV

## Delivered

- Chapter II — **1198 BCE · Salt Under the Stars** — Nara Kesh — 9 rooms — Bronze Star Spear — salt-lens astronomical alignment — The Salt Astronomer.
- Chapter III — **395 CE · The Last Census** — Marcus Aelian — 9 rooms — Census Gladius — three restored citizen identities — The Last Enumerator.
- Chapter IV — **1587 · The Anatomy of a Shadow** — Lucia Varetti — 9 rooms — Anatomist's Rapier — three-stage optical/shadow alignment — The Anatomist's Shadow.
- 27 new authored rooms, 3 bosses, 15 new enemy archetypes/elite patterns, 3 new weapons, checkpoints, secrets and documents.
- Sequential Memory Spine progression in Blackthorn Memory Vault.
- Save schema 7 with chapter progression and secret telemetry.
- New AI modes: ambush, orbit, flee and blink.
- Boss phase III environmental vulnerability hooks.

## Design rule

The three chapters deliberately use different verbs: **align**, **restore**, and **collapse**. Their bosses all share the campaign's readable three-phase combat grammar, but the final phase cannot be won by damage alone.
